# Comparison

## Similar tools

- [WestHealth pdf-form-filler](https://github.com/WestHealth/pdf-form-filler) and [pdfformfiller2](https://github.com/nkitsul/pdfformfiller2) are established AcroForm filling libraries.
- Adobe Fill & Sign is the consumer baseline for filling a form by hand.

## What formwise does differently

- Explainable field-name matching with a small alias table.
- A local review UI with the PDF preview and every value.
- Visible filled output with searchable text, not blank widget appearances.
- An optional encrypted profile with a passphrase.

## Tradeoff

Adobe covers signatures, mobile capture, and hosted workflows. formwise covers the private, repeated AcroForm fill and the review step, and leaves signatures and scanned forms on the roadmap.
